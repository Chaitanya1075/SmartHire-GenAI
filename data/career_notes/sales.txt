SALES - CAREER GUIDE

## Sales Representative
Sales Representatives (often called Sales Development Reps or Account
Executives depending on the stage of the sales cycle they own) generate
leads, pitch products/services, and close deals with customers.
Core skills: communication and persuasion, resilience (handling rejection),
active listening to understand customer needs, and basic pipeline/CRM
management.
Common tools: CRM software (Salesforce, HubSpot), email/outreach tools,
and increasingly LinkedIn Sales Navigator for prospecting.
To grow: track and be able to talk about your numbers (quota attainment,
deals closed, pipeline generated) since sales is one of the most metrics-
driven career paths. Moving from an entry Sales Development Rep role to a
full Account Executive role usually requires demonstrating strong lead
qualification and a track record of hitting targets.

## Account Executive
Account Executives own the full sales cycle for a set of accounts/prospects -
from qualifying interest through negotiating and closing the deal, and often
managing the ongoing client relationship afterward.
Core skills: consultative selling (understanding a client's business problem,
not just pitching features), negotiation, deal strategy, and relationship
management for renewals/upsells.
Common tools: CRM (Salesforce), proposal/quoting tools, and video conferencing
for demos.
To grow: build expertise in the specific industry/product you sell in - deep
domain knowledge (e.g. understanding a client's business model) differentiates
top performers. Developing account planning skills and moving toward larger,
more strategic accounts is a common growth path toward Senior AE or Sales
Manager roles.
